Design Sustainability Manufacturing

Stellum: Sustainable
Manufacturing Plant Design

Engineering Design for Sustainability · University of Wollongong Dubai

Year 2025
Grade 98 / 100
CAPEX $9.5M of $10M
Location Vietnam (design context)
01 — Problem

The Challenge

Design a fully sustainable manufacturing facility within a $10M capital expenditure constraint, targeting production in Vietnam. The design had to satisfy sustainability criteria across the full product lifecycle — from material selection and production efficiency through to end-of-life recyclability — while remaining financially viable.

02 — Approach

Design Approach

The project required simultaneous decision-making across three interlocking domains: factory layout, product engineering, and financial modelling.

Factory Layout: A six-zone layout was designed and produced as a full set of AutoCAD technical drawings. The zones were structured to optimise material flow, minimise transport distances between stations, and reduce energy use per unit produced.

Product Design: A stainless-steel product was selected specifically for its minimal material complexity and full end-of-life recyclability. The material choice was justified through a lifecycle analysis comparing alternatives on sustainability, cost, and manufacturability.

Financial Modelling: The full CAPEX breakdown, NPV calculation, ROI projection, and payback period were developed from first principles. The final design was delivered $500K under budget.

03 — Results

Outcomes

$9.5M delivered under $10M CAPEX
2.2yr payback period
$18.43M NPV
45.46% ROI
98 grade
