About

I am a Mechanical Engineering student at the University of Wollongong Dubai, currently in my second year with a 92 WAM and Dean's List recognition.

My engineering path has taken me from hand-drawn technical drawings at the Karlsruhe Institute of Technology in Germany to data systems engineering at Mercedes-Benz, and from fabricating wind tunnels for aerodynamics research to building computational tools in MATLAB. Each experience has opened a different side of mechanical engineering, and each one has convinced me there is more to explore.

While my focus is firmly within mechanical engineering, I am intentionally exploring several disciplines within the field before specializing. I have pursued opportunities in manufacturing, design, materials, and computational engineering to develop a broad technical foundation and better understand where I can create the greatest impact. My internships, coursework, and personal projects reflect this purposeful approach.
